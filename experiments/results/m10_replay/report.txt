Rolling baseline, alarms after the injection: median 16 s late, max 62 s.

Why synthetic cases alarm early: the triggering columns are mem and rps (alarms_*.csv,
top columns recorded in the diagnostic re-run). pare.synthetic makes mem a random walk and
rps a slow sine load, so their "normal" range keeps growing; any level-based detector learns
too narrow a range from a finite baseline. That is a property of the generator, which is
fixed for the research, not something to tune the detector against. Recorded RCAEval cases
behave like real telemetry and are the ones the replay page recommends.
